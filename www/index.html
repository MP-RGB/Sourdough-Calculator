Method: the levain is ripe when its microbes have regrown to the density of ripe starter. Time = ln(1 ÷ starter fraction) ÷ growth rate, and growth rate comes from published sourdough yeast and lactic-acid-bacteria data (Gänzle). Rates climb about 12% per °C between 14°C and 24°C, but flatten above ~26°C because yeast slows while bacteria speed up. Published data runs 10–40°C; values at 5–8°C are extrapolated.
